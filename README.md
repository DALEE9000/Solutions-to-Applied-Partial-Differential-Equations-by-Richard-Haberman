# Solutions Manual: Applied Partial Differential Equations with Fourier Series and Boundary Value Problems

**Richard Haberman, 5th Edition**

Worked solutions to exercises from Haberman's *Applied Partial Differential Equations*, typeset in LaTeX with TikZ diagrams.

## About

This solutions manual is a personal project developed as part of a self-directed study in partial differential equations, with the intent of doing stronger research at my post in the Oceanography Department at Columbia University's Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory. In my writing, I emphasize rigorous derivation and physical intuition. Solutions include extended commentary on the physical interpretation of results where relevant.

## Building

Compile with `pdflatex` or `latexmk`:

```bash
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -outdir=build apde/apde.tex
```
